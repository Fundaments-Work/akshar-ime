Model: data/akshar_wasm.model — 8.91 MB, 4.92 MB Brotli. The unified container bundles the transliteration model, the 470k-word vocabulary and the reranker, so no separate lexicon is needed. Serve with Content-Encoding: br and a long cache. Learned words persist in localStorage.
